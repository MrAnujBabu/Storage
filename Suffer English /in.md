## 1-Page Executive Summary

**Sleep and food are bidirectional.** St-Onge's lab uniquely runs both directions in controlled inpatient studies.

- **Sex-divergent appetite:** 5 nights of 4h vs 9h time-in-bed → **men ↑ morning ghrelin** (P=0.042), **women ↓ afternoon GLP-1** (P=0.016) [1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23115399). No effect when pooled — prior male-only studies missed this.
  > “in men specifically we saw an increase in ghrelin... in women we saw a reduction in GLP-1... the satiety hormone was reduced as a result of short sleep in women.” (00:02:29)

- **Modest sleep debt → weight gain even isocaloric:** 1.5 h/night reduction (7.5h→6h) for 6 weeks increased insulin resistance and BP, worst in post-menopausal women (00:21:56). In a 4-day isocaloric study, short sleepers lost 0.5 lb *less* fat (1.7 vs 2.2 lb) [2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8591680/). Mechanism = ↓ NEAT + ↓ evening fat oxidation, not ↑ cortisol (cortisol unchanged in lab).

- **Food → Sleep:** Fiber ↑ slow-wave sleep (SWS), saturated fat ↓ SWS, sugar ↑ arousals [3](https://link.springer.com/article/10.5664/jcsm.5384)
  > “higher intakes of fiber were associated with more deep sleep, higher intakes of saturated fat, less deep sleep, and then more refined carbohydrates, simple sugars, more arousals” (00:33:25)

**Core Tools from episode:**
1. **Informed Food Choices (21:56)** – label hunger after poor sleep
2. **Mediterranean/DASH for insomnia (27:59)** – WHI 3-yr data
3. **Early Meals (1:11:23)** – first bite ~1h post-wake, last bite ≥3h pre-bed
4. **Portion & Portfolio Diet (1:22:54)** – soy/legumes + nuts + plant sterols + soluble fiber ≈ statin LDL drop

## 1. CORE MECHANISMS

### Men vs Women
| | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Hormone change | ↑ fasting & morning total ghrelin | ↓ afternoon GLP-1 |
| Brain effect | ↑ reward-center activation to food cues | ↓ satiety signaling (vagus→NTS) |
| Subjective | “drive to eat” ↑ | “brake on eating” ↓ |

> Prior studies missed this because “all the prior studies had been done in men only” (00:04:10)

### 45–90 min nightly debt → weight gain WITHOUT extra calories
- **Lab finding:** Wakefulness costs ~90 kcal/night (5% ↑ EE), but ad lib intake ↑ ~300 kcal → net +210 kcal
- **Free-living:** 5h vs 7.5h for 2 weeks → +0.5 kg fat “you do nothing and you just... sleep less and gain almost a pound” (00:13:20)
- **Why:** ↓ spontaneous movement (“more sedentary during the day. Less spontaneous activity” – 00:12:39), ↓ fat oxidation with late meals, insulin resistance shifts fuel to storage

## 2. FOOD → SLEEP EVIDENCE

**St-Onge 2016 JCSM study design** [3](https://link.springer.com/article/10.5664/jcsm.5384):
- n=26, 30-45y, randomized crossover
- 5 nights 9h TIB vs 4h TIB
- Days 1-4: controlled diet (31% fat, 53% carb, 17% protein)
- Day 5: ad libitum
- Polysomnography nightly

**Results on ad lib day:**
- Sleep latency +70%
- SWS −20%
- **Fiber** predicted more SWS (P=0.0286), less stage 1
- **Saturated fat %** predicted less SWS (P=0.0422)
- **Sugar/other carbs** predicted more arousals (P=0.032)

## 4. ACTIONABLE PROTOCOLS

**Tool: Informed Food Choices (21:56)**
> “if you step back and think that maybe part of it is because you didn't sleep well... then you can make your appropriate choices”

**Tool: Mediterranean/DASH (27:59)**
- Daily: vegetables, fruit, nuts/seeds, olive oil
- ≥4x/week legumes, 2x/week fish
- Low-fat dairy (DASH), limit red meat, sugar, salt

**Tool: Early Meals (1:11:23)**
- First calories: **~1 hour after waking**
- Last calories: **≥3 hours before bed** (“I personally like to eat my last meal at least three hours before going to bed” – 00:33:25)
- Window: ~10 hours (e.g., 7:30am–5:30pm)

**Tool: Portion & Portfolio (1:22:54)**
- Portfolio: 25g soy/legume protein + 30g nuts + 2g plant sterols + 10g soluble fiber daily
- Plate: ½ non-starchy veg, ¼ whole grain/legume, ¼ protein

---

## 5. MEAL TIMING WINDOWS

- **Early (8am-6pm):** ↑ fat oxidation in metabolic chamber
- **Late (12pm-10pm):** same calories → ↓ fat oxidation
- **Shift work:** eat with biological morning, avoid large meals before day-sleep
- **Naps (40:52):** ≤30-60 min, early afternoon, >8h before bedtime
- **Exercise:** St-Onge combines morning run + yoga; avoid vigorous <2h before bed

## 6. CRITICAL ANALYSIS

**Limitations:**
- Industry sponsorship (1:41:20): corn chips, coffee oligos, MCT. St-Onge: “right to publish... in the contract,” but null studies often rejected → publication bias
- Lab cortisol unchanged – may not reflect real-life stress
- MESA/WHI observational – residual confounding (activity, social)

**Huberman nuance:**
- Expected cortisol rise – surprised by null
- Personally avoids seed oils; St-Onge neutral: “I'm a fan of every liquid oil... oils that remain liquid at room temperature”

**Adaptations:**
- **(a) Indian vegetarian:** Keep dal, vegetables, curd; shift dinner earlier, replace late ghee with mustard/peanut oil, add 30g nuts, ginger tea, 10-h window
- **(b) Weight loss:** Early 10-h window + 30g fiber + 1 tbsp MCT *replace* + ginger pre-lunch + plan 300 kcal buffer after short sleep
- **(c) Insomnia:** Mediterranean/DASH, ≥25g fiber, <7% sat fat, zero sugar after 4pm, finish dinner 3h pre-bed
